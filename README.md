**VELDARA** is a travel poster for a place that isn't there: an island that floats above a sea of clouds at sunset. A lit lighthouse and a small cottage stand on its golden grass; a waterfall spills off its edge and vanishes into the clouds below, while a second, smaller islet drifts in the haze behind it. The whole image is drawn in code — gradients, shapes and hand-built letterforms, rendered to a PNG by a tiny procedural engine with its own PNG encoder. No images, no network, no libraries.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the poster to `/output/entry.png` (or `./output/entry.png` when `/output` is not writable). The output is a 1024×1024 PNG, well under the 1 MiB limit. Runtime is under a second; the picture is deterministic — the same poster every run.

## The place

Veldara has no map, no harbour, and no road in — you arrive by watching for its light. The poster promises exactly one evening there: warm wind off the cloud sea, the sun low and huge, and the lighthouse beam sweeping out over nothing at all.
